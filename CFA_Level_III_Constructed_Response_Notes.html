简易得分答案与可复用英文句式

高频英文句式 机制、因果与比较 适配性与客户约束

主观题速答笔记

只保留可以在考场中直接调用的简洁表达。

机制、因果与比较

This reflects a systematic process rather than discretionary judgment.

句型：This reflects [A] rather than [B].

Higher reinvestment rates result in higher long-term earnings.

句型：Higher [cause] results in higher/lower [effect].

Holdings-based analysis is less appropriate because quarter-end holdings may not represent exposures during the quarter.

句型：[Method] is less appropriate because [limitation].

The yield on commercial paper will increase, while the fixed borrowing cost remains unchanged.

句型：[A] will increase/decrease, while [B] remains unchanged.

适配性与客户约束

The endowment model typically allocates a substantial portion of the portfolio to alternative investments and relies on active management.

用途：概括 endowment model 的典型资产配置与管理方式。

This approach is inconsistent with the client’s preference for [preferred approach].

用途：说明某项投资方法与客户明确偏好不一致。

[Investment] is often illiquid, whereas the client requires liquidity to meet [cash needs].

用途：对比投资工具的流动性特征与客户的现金需求。
